# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must be respectful, constructive, and professional. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome when it remains focused on observable behavior, source, tests, tradeoffs, and project scope.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/pcmbezerra). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be reviewed in good faith with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing PRDC or its community.
